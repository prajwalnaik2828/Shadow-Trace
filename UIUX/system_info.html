Understanding Performance Metrics

These metrics show how accurately each AI detection model performs when tested against files where the ground truth is known. Precision measures how often the model is correct when it raises an alert. Recall measures how often the model catches actual steganography. F1 Score balances both. Higher is better for all three. ROC-AUC above 0.5 is better than random. 1.0 is perfect.

INTERPRETATION

The ensemble achieves its best performance when all four components are active. Removing the statistical analysis (Chi-Square and RS) causes the largest drop in F1 score indicating these mathematical tests provide signal that the neural models alone do not capture. The ALASKA2 adaptive model has the lowest individual AUC at 0.54 which reflects the fundamental difficulty of detecting adaptive steganography that is specifically designed to be undetectable by statistical methods.
